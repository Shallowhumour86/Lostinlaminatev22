## Endings / spoiler walkthrough

**A Sky of Your Own**: Take the travel card from the hotel case. Restore identity at the civic archive. Accept Quinn's job in the concourse, bring the parcel to Dahlia in the garden, and pay the forty-credit hotel debt through Cor at the terminal. Use the orbital elevator and leave.

**Nothing Lost**: Restore identity. Accept the offworld researcher's capsule in the concourse and deliver it to the archive dropbox. Examine the garden pool for proof of the forged booking. Submit the evidence at the embassy terminal to cancel the charge. Use the orbital elevator and choose to publish the evidence.

**A Different Kind of Dawn**: Restore identity and complete Quinn's delivery to Dahlia. Ask Sister Euphoria about staying, then accept the caretaker role. The garden settles your account as part of its welcome; your identity and travel rights remain intact.

Quests pay only once, and accepting a quest repeatedly does not duplicate its item. Paying debt is optional; the embassy route preserves your credits. No selection requires a timed response. There is no irreversible failure before choosing an ending.
